# Midnight Courier

One package. Ridiculously fragile. Two minutes to get it down the hill.

A downhill bike game set on Lantern Hill, a rainy neighbourhood that falls away from a noodle shop at the top
to the Hotel Marlowe on the harbour. You can bomb down staircases, thread an alley and jump the canal to beat
the clock, but every hard landing rattles the antique glass on your back. The tip at the door pays for speed
*and* for care, so the fastest route isn't always the winning one. `R` restarts instantly, and your best run
rides beside you as a ghost.

Built in C++ on Unreal Engine 5.9. Every model, the whole neighbourhood, every material and every sound is
generated by scripts in this folder.

## Build and play

    ./build.sh            # compile (also builds all assets the first time)
    ./build.sh --assets   # regenerate the bike, the city, the audio and the Unreal content
    ./play.sh             # play in a 1600x900 window (extra args pass through)

Needs the source-built engine at `~/UnrealEngine` (override with `UE_ENGINE_DIR`) and Blender 5.x at
`/Applications/Blender.app` (override with `BLENDER`; only for `--assets`).

## How it plays

- **The route.** Crown Street, the Kiln Road hairpin, Market Street, Tanner Row, Lamp Hill, the canal, Gull
  Bridge and Pier Lane is the safe way down (about 1:35 if you ride it well). Three shortcuts cut it to about a
  minute: **Lantern Steps** (four flights of stairs), **Needle Alley** (narrow, two short stair sets) and the
  **dock jump** across the canal (you need about 12 m/s off the kicker; less and you swim).
- **The package** rides in a carrier on your back. Its condition drops when the wheels strike kerbs and steps,
  when your legs bottom out on a landing, when you hit a wall, and a lot when you crash or go in the water.
  At 0% the run is over.
- **Brace** (hold Space) and you stand on the pedals: your legs soak up three to four times more, you can't
  pedal and you steer a little wider. Press it in the last moment before touchdown for a **feather landing**,
  which cuts that landing's damage to a fifth. Release it to **hop** (hold longer, hop higher): hop kerbs,
  hop off the top of a flight, re-brace before you land.
- **Power slide** (Shift) drops rear grip so you can swing the back out through the hairpin and the corners.
  It scrubs speed, so use it to turn, not to slow down.
- **The tip.** $20 plus $1 per second to spare, times a care multiplier (condition^1.5), plus $15 for a
  pristine box (95%+) or $6 for a careful one (85%+). Grades: S $85, A $65, B $48, C $32.
- **Checkpoints** at Market Street, Lamp Hill and Pier Lane show your split against your best run. Crash and
  you are put back a few seconds earlier; the clock keeps running.
- Stop in the glowing ring at the Hotel Marlowe to hand the package over.

## Layout

**Game code** (`Source/MidnightCourier/`)
- `McBike.*` — the bike. Hand-rolled physics at a fixed 240 Hz: a rigid body in the vertical plane (height and
  pitch) on two sphere-swept wheels with spring/damper suspension and rim strikes, an arcade grip/slide model on
  the ground plane, a swept capsule for walls, air control, a second spring for the rider's legs that carries the
  package, and the damage model. Also the procedural rider (two-bone IK to the pedals and bars), wheel spray and
  the chase camera.
- `McGame.*` — run flow (title, countdown, riding, respawn, delivered, failed), input, scoring, checkpoints and
  splits, the ghost (recorded at 30 Hz, saved to `Saved/Courier/best.run`), the autopilot and test hooks.
- `McWorld.*` — builds the level from `Content/Courier/Data/level.json`: city chunks, lights, fog, sky, GPU rain,
  the drop-off beacon, lightning, post-processing.
- `McHud.*` — canvas HUD and screens. `McAudio.*` — loops driven by speed/slip, impacts, the package's glassy
  complaints, music.

**Art** (`Art/`)
- `mc/kit.py` — mesh builder (boxes, tubes, text, facing-aware polygons) and FBX export.
- `build_bike.py` — bike, rider parts, package, rain mesh, beacon.
- `build_city.py` — the neighbourhood: streets from straight/arc paths, Lantern Steps, Needle Alley, the canal,
  bridge, dock kicker, harbour, ~2,600 buildings (frontage + block fill), neon signs, shopfronts, lamps,
  lanterns, a gap-filling floor; writes `level.json` (start, checkpoints, water, lights, autopilot routes,
  minimap).

**Scripts**
- `Scripts/import_assets.py` — builds all materials as procedural HLSL graphs (wet asphalt with puddles and rain
  ripples, windows, neon, water, GPU rain, ghost, sky) and imports meshes and audio. `MC_ONLY=materials,meshes,
  city,audio,data` limits it.
- `Scripts/make_audio.py` — synthesizes every sound and both music loops with numpy.
- `Scripts/dev.sh` — `build`, `sync`, `py <script>`, `game [args]`.

## Test flags

Pass to `./play.sh` or `Scripts/dev.sh game`:

- `-McAuto=main` / `-McAuto=short` — autopilot rides the safe route or all the shortcuts.
- `-McScript=throttle@0:5,steer=-0.5@2:1,brace@4:0.3` — scripted input (times from the start of the ride).
- `-McTele=x,y,z,yaw` — start somewhere else (cm, degrees). `-McCam=x,y,z,pitch,yaw` — fixed camera.
- `-McShots=name@seconds,...` — screenshots to `Saved/Shots/`. `-McQuit=seconds`.
- `-McTelemetry` logs `MC_TEL` lines; `-LogCmds="LogMc Verbose"` adds every shock and landing.
- `-McInvincible`, `-McFresh` (ignore the saved best), `-McNoGhost`, `-McMute`, `-McRide` (skip the title),
  `-McExposure=<ev>`, `-McTestCourse` (physics test lot instead of the city).

The log prints `MC_RESULT` at the end of each run and `MC_PERF` (frame rate) every five seconds.
