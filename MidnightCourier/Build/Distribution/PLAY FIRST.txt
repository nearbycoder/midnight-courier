MIDNIGHT COURIER
One package. Ridiculously fragile. Two minutes to get it down the hill.

Requirements: a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 14 or later.

First launch: the app is signed ad hoc, not notarized. macOS will refuse to open it the first time.
Right-click (or Control-click) "Midnight Courier.app", choose Open, then Open again.
If macOS still blocks it: System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway".

Controls (keyboard / gamepad)
  W / RT ........ pedal                 S / LT ......... brake
  A D / stick ... steer                 SPACE / A ...... hold to brace, release to hop
  SHIFT / X ..... power slide           R / Y .......... instant restart
  Esc / Start ... pause and settings    G / Back ....... ghost on or off
In the air, W and S tilt the bike.

Brace in the last moment before you land for a feather landing.
The tip pays for speed and for care: the fastest route isn't always the winning one.
